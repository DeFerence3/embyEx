Round 1 · three layouts for the player

Palette, shapes and the system UI font are fixed by the approved Marquee direction, so these three differ in layout, control shape, timeline style and subtitle typeface. In all three the timeline is full width with only the two timers beside it, every other control sits below it, and every control shows its shortcut.

A · Centre Stage

Today's player with the timeline freed. PiP and Fullscreen leave the timeline and drop into a row under it, joined by volume and the track buttons. Fullscreen keeps the big centre Play you already know; windowed moves every control under the picture.

Trade-off: the transport sits in the centre in fullscreen but in the row when windowed, and the margins left and right of the windowed picture stay empty.

Mood: familiar, quiet, cinematic · Controls: tonal 48 px circles, 80 px Play · Timeline: Material slider, white over video · Subtitles: Atkinson Hyperlegible

C · Console

A desk player. An opaque console shows the current subtitle, audio and speed as labelled values instead of bare icons, and windowed mode turns the empty margin into a Chapters and Up next panel.
