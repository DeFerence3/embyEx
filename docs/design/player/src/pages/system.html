Design system

Dockside: B's floating dock with C's heading and panel. The picture comes first, every control lives in an opaque pill, and every action has a key.

Color

The Marquee palette from the Details design, unchanged. Three roles were added for chrome over video: video, on-video and video-scrim-floor (the lightest point under text on a white frame).

Contrast (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Type

System font for every piece of UI. Subtitles are the one exception and use a bundled typeface (Nunito by default).

Spacing and shape

Icons

Material Symbols Rounded, filled.

Icon button

Round tonal on its own; inside a pill the pill is the container. Every icon button has a label and, where one exists, its key.

48 × 48 px target, 8 px apart. Press turns the circle into a squircle.

Play

The one filled control. Its shape tells paused from playing, so the state never depends on colour.

64 px in the dock, 56 px on phone landscape, 48 px in PiP.

Pills and dock

Opaque, so they read over any picture without a gradient.

Timeline

Timers left and right only. Chapter ticks ahead of the playhead. Slider role with a spoken value that includes the chapter.

Tooltips and key caps

The tooltip appears after 0.8 s on hover and at once on keyboard focus.

Shortcut feedback

Status pill at the top centre of the picture.

Menu

Opens upwards from its pill. On phone the same rows come as a bottom sheet.

Panel

Chapters and Up next. The list is one Tab stop; arrow keys move inside it.

Up next card

The only thing allowed over the windowed picture, and only in the last 10 seconds.

Phone tiles

Replace today's scrolling row of icons with labelled tiles.

Subtitles

Typeface, size and background are set on the Subtitle style page.

Error and feedback framework

Decision order: needs a decision or is costly → T6; tied to one input → T1; content can't show → T5; lasting condition → T4; otherwise an event → T3.

T1 · Field error

Tab

Tab moves between controls, so it can't be a shortcut. Press another key.

T2 (form summary) is not used: the player has no long forms.
