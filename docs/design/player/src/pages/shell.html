Behaviour rules

Controls hideFullscreen and phone landscape: after 3 s while playing (longer with the system's accessibility timeout). Windowed, portrait and PiP-hover: never while the pointer or focus is inside.
Controls stayPaused, buffering, failed, a menu or sheet open, keyboard focus inside the controls, pointer resting on them, seeking.
Focus on openThe player itself, not a control. Space is play / pause until Tab moves into the controls; then Space presses the focused control. K and the media key always play / pause.
Shortcuts while hiddenWork without showing the controls; only the feedback pill appears. Tab shows the controls and focuses Back.
MenusArrow keys move, Enter picks and closes, typing a letter jumps. Focus returns to the button that opened the menu.
Reduced motionFlat timeline, no shape morph, no feedback fade, static glow, no Up next auto-advance.
Screen readerControls never hide. The timeline speaks time and chapter. Feedback pills and Up next are announced. No auto-advance.
Getting readyNo blocking screen before playback: the player opens at once with the artwork and a spinner in place of Play.
Saving your placeClosing returns at once. A small pill appears only if saving takes longer than 300 ms. Failure is a T3 with Retry on the screen you return to.
Narrow windowUnder 1100 px the panel is closed by default and opens over the picture. Under 940 px the volume slider folds into its button, Speed and PiP move into More.
