Shell & behaviour

How the details screen sits in the app and behaves across input methods, window sizes and system settings.

Window sizes

Layout follows the window width, never the platform name. A desktop window dragged narrow gets the phone layout; a tablet in landscape gets the desktop layout.

  • Compact < 700: artwork on top fading into the page, single column, full-width Play, three equal action tiles.
  • Medium 700–1099: taller artwork, head column max 640, Play sized to content, Media info as a side column.
  • Expanded ≥ 1100: artwork bleeds right, head column on a left scrim, body in two columns. Text never sits on less than a fully opaque scrim.

Keyboard, remote and screen reader

  • Opening details puts focus on Play (desktop, TV remote) so Enter starts watching; screen readers start at the title heading.
  • Tab / Shift+Tab follow the numbered order below. Arrow keys move inside the action row, chip row and rails. Esc closes a menu/sheet, otherwise goes back.
  • Rails scroll the focused card into view with scroll-margin so focus is never hidden at an edge (WCAG 2.4.11).
  • Toggles announce state ("Favorite, toggle, on"); the download tile announces progress ("Downloading, 42%") and updates politely, at most every 10%.

Motion

  • Expressive spring on Play press (shape squish) and on toggles (fill morph). Hero artwork fades in over 300 ms; no parallax.
  • With reduced motion: springs become 150 ms fades, the artwork appears without fade, progress rings update without animation.
